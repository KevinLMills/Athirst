How to play

  • Your people need water. Every person drinks from the water bank each turn. If it runs dry, people start to die.
  • Assign workers. Use the + / − buttons on a site to move people there. Unassigned people default to Water Collection.
  • Grow. When you bank enough surplus water in a turn, your population grows. New occupation sites unlock at population milestones.
  • Research and Production each build up along their own progress track. Reaching a milestone unlocks a permanent upgrade — better tools, easier growth, and eventually new occupations.
  • Build the Water Spout. It's a Production milestone — once reached, water becomes limitless and everyone on Water Collection is free to be reassigned. This is the win condition, but the game keeps going.
  • Press End Turn to advance time and see the results.
